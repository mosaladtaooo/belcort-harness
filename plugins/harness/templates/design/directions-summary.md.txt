## How to pick

Open each HTML file in your browser, click around, and pick the one that *feels* right — not the one that looks most polished, the one whose intent matches what you want. Reply in chat with one of:

- `I pick direction-1` (or `direction-2` or `direction-3`) — the orchestrator will re-dispatch Designer to produce a hi-fi self-contained HTML prototype of the chosen direction.
- `/harness:design reroll "<your feedback>"` — if none of the three fit. Be specific about what's off ("too cold", "second one had energy I want, but with the first's restraint", "all three felt corporate") — vague feedback ("don't like any of them") makes the next round a coin flip.
- `cancel` — stop here, leave the directions on disk.
